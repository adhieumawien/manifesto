The vision

A constituency where people can live with dignity and hope.

Awul and Manalor deserve representation that listens to communities and turns their priorities into practical action.

Years of insecurity, limited public services, economic hardship and humanitarian emergencies have placed heavy pressure on families across Warrap. Our response must bring together peacebuilding, stronger public services, livelihood opportunities and accountable leadership.

Samuel Adhieu Mawien Arou's proposed vision is rooted in a simple principle: public office is a responsibility to serve, not a privilege to enjoy.

The policy agenda

Ten priorities.
One shared future.

A people-centred agenda for Awul and Manalor, with a wider commitment to Tonj North County and Warrap State.

01✳

Peace & civilian protection

Support dialogue, early warning, lawful justice and cooperation between communities and security institutions.

  • Community peace consultations
  • Prevention of revenge attacks
  • Protection of women and children
02↗

Education for every child

Promote school access, teacher support and practical measures to keep girls and vulnerable children learning.

  • Girls' education and retention
  • Safer learning environments
  • Skills and alternative learning
03＋

Healthcare & nutrition

Advocate for stronger primary healthcare, maternal and child services, nutrition support and referrals.

  • Assess gaps in Farasika and beyond
  • Essential medicines and outreach
  • Nutrition screening and referral
04⌁

Food security & farming

Connect urgent humanitarian needs with agricultural recovery, livestock health and resilient livelihoods.

  • Farm inputs and extension support
  • Veterinary outreach
  • Household livelihood recovery
05⌖

Roads, water & sanitation

Promote phased, needs-based improvements to feeder roads, safe water points and sanitation.

  • Access to schools and health centres
  • Functional, maintained water points
  • Climate-aware infrastructure
06◇

Youth skills & jobs

Expand practical pathways to training, apprenticeships, entrepreneurship and constructive civic participation.

  • Vocational and digital skills
  • Small business development
  • Youth-led peacebuilding
07♡

Women & child protection

Promote equal participation, protection services and dignity for women, children and vulnerable groups.

  • Prevention of gender-based violence
  • Support for persons with disabilities
  • Inclusive community decisions
08⚖

Justice & human rights

Improve legal awareness, access to justice and fair treatment through lawful, accountable institutions.

  • Legal awareness and referrals
  • Fair and timely investigations
  • Rights-respecting dispute resolution
09↗

Local economy & markets

Strengthen market access and opportunities for farmers, cattle keepers, traders and local businesses.

  • Producer groups and cooperatives
  • Local value chains
  • Opportunities for women and youth

Accountable representation

Keep constituents informed, raise local priorities and monitor public commitments and project progress.

  • Regular community consultations
  • Public progress updates
  • Transparent, needs-based investment

People first

Protect lives today.
Build resilience for tomorrow.

Families affected by conflict, displacement, hunger and malnutrition need timely, impartial support. Humanitarian assistance must be based on assessed needs, never on political affiliation.

Alongside emergency response, communities need support to restore livelihoods, access services and prepare for future shocks.

A practical approach to representation, grounded in community consultation and public accountability.

First 100 days

Listen and set priorities

02

First year

Advocate and engage institutions

Raise constituency priorities through appropriate parliamentary channels and seek their inclusion in relevant plans and budgets.

About the candidate

Samuel Adhieu
Mawien Arou

Aspirant for Member of State Parliament, Constituency No. 19, Awul and Manalor Payams, Tonj North County, Warrap State.

“I am a son of this land. I know our villages, the sacrifices of our mothers, the wisdom of our elders, and the dreams of our children.”

Driven by a deep commitment to his people, Samuel Adhieu Mawien Arou aspires to represent Awul and Manalor with integrity, humility, and a genuine spirit of service. His professional interests span the rule of law, access to justice, political science, governance, protection, and human rights.

Samuel believes that leadership is not about personal recognition, but about listening to communities, protecting their dignity, and ensuring their voices are heard where decisions are made.

His vision is of a constituency where peace is stronger than conflict, education opens doors for every child, healthcare is accessible, and young people have opportunities to build meaningful futures.

Through community consultation, peaceful coexistence, accountable leadership, and practical advocacy, he seeks to bridge the gap between the people of Awul and Manalor and decision-makers at county, state, and national levels.

“We cannot rewrite yesterday, but together, we can take responsibility for tomorrow.”
